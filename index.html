Project 04 · Lead hub

Fair leads for a whole
sales team. Automatically.

My roleDesigned and built end to endToolsNode.js, GoHighLevel APIUsers12+ reps and admins

30leads this round
12 reps · 2 each + merit

A tool built into the CRM for a team of 12+ reps. Reps request leads and admins approve them. A timed lead pool deals leads out evenly in rounds, with bonus leads for the reps who close.

  • 9 PM hygiene check. Every night it flags any pipeline that isn't kept clean.
  • Five flags block new leads until the rep fixes their pipeline.
  • Callback limits stop leads piling up unworked.
  • Throttled API use so the CRM is never overloaded.

About me

I build for salespeople because I am one. I close high-ticket solar and battery deals by phone, text and in person, so I know where a deal actually gets lost.

My path

I studied Civil Engineering at Western Sydney University, then went into sales and never looked back: first face-to-face sales at Creative Activation, then high-ticket deals at a national solar and battery retailer from 2024. In 2025 I got into AI. It started with automating my own sales work and grew into building complete CRM systems and AI agents for whole teams.

How I work

I start with a scoping call, design the solution, build it in GoHighLevel, n8n or code, ship it, then keep improving it on live feedback. I use Claude Code every day to build faster.

Selling and building

I still do both every day. Selling keeps my builds honest, and building makes my selling faster. That's the mix I bring to a team.

What I'm looking for

A role where I build AI automation that real teams depend on, and talk to the people who use it. Remote or Sydney.
